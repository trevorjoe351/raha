Our Story

A Taste of the Kenyan Coast

At Raha ya Pwani, we bring the warmth of the Swahili Coast straight to Rwaka. From fragrant biryanis to crispy viazi karai, every dish is crafted with coastal love — bold spices, fresh ingredients, and recipes passed down through generations. Come as you are; leave as family.
